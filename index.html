    ABOUT THE DEMO

    Policy-to-Patient Intelligence

    Coverwise is a hackathon prototype for exploring policy wording and treatment costs.

    The built-in policy and cost data are fictional. Uploaded text can be searched with page-level citations, but the estimator still uses sample policy terms and regional cost ranges. Scanned PDFs need OCR, which is not included.

    Use the checklist to prepare questions for a hospital or insurer. This prototype does not make coverage decisions.
